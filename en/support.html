Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Visualia works without an account and without giving any personal data.

Where is my progress kept?

On your iPhone, and only there. It does not leave your device and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, it is erased.

How do I erase my data?

Your progress, from Settings, under Privacy, with “Erase my progress”. To erase everything, including your name and your character, uninstall the app.

I changed iPhone or reinstalled the app. Do I get back what I bought?

Yes. Open the Plus tab and tap “Restore purchases”. What you bought is in your App Store account, not on the phone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name and then Subscriptions. Do it at least 24 hours before the period ends. You keep Plus until the end of what you already paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A sign is not the one I know.

That can happen: sign language varies from place to place and from person to person. Tell us, with the sign and where you use it, and we will look into it.

Can children use it?

Yes. The “Kids” path is meant to be used with an adult, and it has no purchases, no rays and no community.

Does it work offline?

Almost all of it, yes. Now and then it needs a connection to confirm the date, which the battery and the streak depend on, and to buy.

I am left-handed. Can I see it the other way round?

Yes: mirror mode turns the tutor round so you sign with your own hand.
