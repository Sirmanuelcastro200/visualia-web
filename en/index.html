Learn sign language with animated tutors

Visualia teaches you Spanish Sign Language (LSE) from scratch, with animated tutors who sign in front of you and lessons that fit into a spare moment.

Coming soon to the App Store For iPhone. Free, with in-app purchases.

How you learn

A tutor who signs for you

Every sign, with the shape of the hand, where it is made and how it moves. Pause it, watch it slowly or mirrored, as many times as you like.

Now you

Your own character makes the sign with you so you can repeat it together. Then short questions to check it has stuck.

A course that makes sense

More than 250 signs and four paths: step by step, as a family, for kids and for work. Each level is a mosaic you uncover lesson by lesson.

So it stays with you

A daily streak with rewards, review of what you find hardest, a speller and a dictionary with every sign.

Made to fit you

Eight tutors, two of them mascots, and your own character. For right-handed and left-handed people.

Nothing depends on sound

Everything can be seen and, on the phone, felt. No account, no advertising and no tracking.

Inside

Free and Plus

Visualia is free to download. Without paying you have the first level of each path, the whole dictionary, review and the speller. Visualia Plus opens the rest of the levels and the review of your mistakes, and removes the daily limit. You can have it with a single payment, for good, or with a monthly or yearly subscription.

About the signs

Sign language is not universal: Visualia teaches the Spanish one (LSE). Like any living language it varies from place to place, and a sign may differ from the one used around you. Visualia is a help to get started: it does not replace a course with Deaf teachers or contact with people who sign. The signs were prepared by consulting public databases of Spanish Sign Language.
