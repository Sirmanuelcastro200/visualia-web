1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is the owner of Visualia, whose name and contact details are shown on the app’s page in the store you downloaded it from. You can write to us at mg.castrozam@gmail.com.

2. In a few words

Visualia does not ask you for an account, shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking tools and sells no data. Almost everything it knows about you stays on your device.

3. What data is processed and where it is

On your device, and only there: the name you type, your character, the country you choose, your path and your goal, your progress, your streak, your rays, your plan and your settings. They do not leave your device and we cannot see them.

Purchases: they are handled by the store (Apple or Google), which processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your card details. The app only receives from the store the confirmation of what you bought.

Date: the battery and the streak change each day, and so that they do not depend on the date set on the device the app checks the time on the internet when it opens. That request carries nothing about you; as with any connection, whoever receives it sees the IP address it comes from. Today it goes to servers run by Google, Apple or Cloudflare. We keep nothing from it.

Reminders: if you turn them on, they are scheduled on your own device. We use no server to send them.

Visualia does not ask for access to your camera, your microphone, your contacts or your location.

4. What for, and on what legal basis

To give you the service you asked for: keeping your progress and your preferences and giving you access to what you bought. The basis is the performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation).

The date check, to prevent fraudulent use. The basis is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)).

Reminders, with your consent, which you give by turning them on and withdraw by turning them off (Article 6(1)(a)).

5. How long it is kept and how to erase it

What is on your device is kept until you erase it.

Your progress (lessons, stars, streak and points) can be erased at any time from Settings, under Privacy, with “Erase my progress”.

To erase everything, including your name, your character and your settings, uninstall the app: when you do, the system erases everything it kept.

Your purchases are not erased that way, because they are not in the app but in your store account. If you install Visualia again, you get them back with “Restore purchases”.

6. Who it is shared with

We do not share your personal data with anyone and we do not sell it.

The only ones who receive anything are the store, when you buy, and the servers that give the time, as explained above. Apple, Google and Cloudflare handle it under their own privacy policies, which offer at least the same protection as this one and explain where they process data and with what safeguards when that is outside the European Economic Area.

If we ever work with another provider that could access your data, it will only be one that commits to protecting it at least as this policy says, and we will say so here first.

7. Children

Visualia can be used without giving any personal data. In Spain, anyone under 14 needs the consent of their mother, father or guardian for their data to be processed; in other countries the age may be different.

On the “Kids” path there are no purchases, no rays and no community.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to correct it, to erase it, to object to its processing, to ask for it to be restricted and to data portability, and to withdraw at any time any consent you have given.

Because your data is on your device, you can see it, change it and erase it yourself from the app, as section 5 explains. You withdraw your consent to reminders by turning them off in Settings or in the notification settings of your device. For any other request, write to us at mg.castrozam@gmail.com.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the data protection authority of your country.

9. Security

What Visualia keeps stays in the space reserved for the app on your device, protected by the system itself. Looking after access to your device is up to you.

10. The website

The Visualia website only gives information: it uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking tools and no forms, and it loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: like any server, GitHub receives the IP address of whoever visits it and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that data.

11. What comes later

If Visualia later offers accounts, a community or backups, this policy will change to explain what is kept and where before you are asked to use them.

Any other important change will be announced in the app.
